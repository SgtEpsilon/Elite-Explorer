Data Sources & Credits
Guardian site point-of-interest layouts (relic towers, obelisks, caskets, tablets, orbs, urns, totems, and pylons) are built from surveyed geometry originally published by the SrvSurvey project (GPL-3.0 — same license as Elite Explorer), converted into our own schema and rendering pipeline. Known site locations are seeded from the same source and refined by your own journal sightings as you visit them. Canonn Research's public API (docs.canonn.tech) is used as a fallback for site types not yet in our local dataset. Our thanks to both projects and the commanders who contributed that data. See ATTRIBUTION.md for full details.
